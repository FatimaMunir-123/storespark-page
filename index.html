How It Works

Three simple steps

This is how StoreSpark AI is planned to work. The first versions are expected to be a guided, manual process before any automation is added.

1

Enter your product details

Add the product name, confirmed features, customer benefits, target audience, and brand voice once.

2

Prepare your content

Choose the kinds of content you need. Drafts are prepared from your details for each channel you select.

3

Review and export

Check every fact, edit the wording, then copy or export the content. You stay responsible for what you publish.

About

Making ecommerce content easier for small sellers

Many small sellers write every listing, caption, and script on their own, one at a time. It is slow, and it takes time away from running the business.

StoreSpark AI is an early stage project with one mission: help small sellers start from a single product brief and get organised draft content for the places they sell and promote.

The product is still being designed and built, and this website will be updated honestly as it grows.

Seller facts come first Content should be based on details the seller provides, with gaps flagged rather than guessed.
People review everything Drafts are a starting point. Sellers check and approve before anything is published.
Honest about limits No promises about sales, rankings, or advertising results.

Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated 31 October 2026. This is a short, plain language notice for an early stage project.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, you fill in a Google Form. It can ask for your name, email address, what you sell, the platform you sell on, the content that takes you the most time, and your agreement to be contacted. You decide what to answer. Questions you leave blank are not collected.

Why we collect it

To tell you when early access opens, and to learn what small sellers need.

Where it is stored

Your answers are stored in Google Forms, under the Google account of the person running StoreSpark AI. Google handles them under its own terms. This website does not run its own database.

Who sees it

Only the person running StoreSpark AI can see the answers. We do not sell your details.

This website

This website has no accounts, no payments, and no advertising trackers. The company that hosts it may keep basic technical records, such as the time of a visit.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your answers at any time. Use the contact details in the Contact section of this website.

This notice is not legal advice and may change as the project grows.
